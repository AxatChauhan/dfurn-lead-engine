## 1. Context & Strategic Assessment

D'furn possesses authentic, heavyweight industrial capabilities that 95% of regional furniture dealers and local workshops cannot match:
* **30-Year Manufacturing Legacy:** Founded in 1991 (originally Devanshi Furniture).
* **Massive Dual-Core Production Capacity:** A 35,000 sq. ft. campus in Kheda processing **750 metric tons of mild steel** and **48,000 sq. ft. of engineered wood** monthly, supported by a 200+ workforce.
* **Tier-1 Blue-Chip Client Roster:** Verified installations for **ISRO, Reliance, Torrent Pharma, Zydus, Ford, Suzuki, Bank of Baroda, and LIC**.

Yet, despite this manufacturing power, D'furn's digital storefront (`dfurn.in`) currently fails to generate qualified inbound commercial pipeline. It suffers from critical website bugs, placeholder text, broken technical tags, zero lead-capture funnels, and a diluted brand positioning (*"From Boardroom to Backyard"*).

This master strategy outlines a clear, repeatable system to transform D'furn into Western India's preeminent contract modular furniture manufacturer.

## 5. Running List of Critical Questions for Business Leadership

To fine-tune operational execution, the growth team requires alignment on the following strategic decisions:

1. **Available Production Bandwidth:**
   * Of the 750 metric tons steel and 48,000 sq. ft. panel monthly capacity, what percentage is currently under committed contract vs. available for new B2B commercial accounts?
2. **Dispatch Window Feasibility:**
   * Can D'furn's Kheda plant realistically commit to a **14-day guaranteed dispatch** for standardized linear workstations (20 to 100 seats), or is a 21-day timeline safer for factory scheduling?
3. **Architect Trade Terms Policy:**
   * Does D'furn have an established policy for architect trade discounts, referral commissions, or project rebates? What specific percentage range (e.g. 3%–7%) does management approve for studio relationships?
4. **Geographic Focus Sequencing:**
   * Should the Q1 expansion remain hyper-focused on Ahmedabad, GIFT City, and Gujarat, or should we allocate 30% of paid ad budget to capture Pune and Mumbai commercial fitout contractors immediately?
5. **Brand Decoupling (Commercial vs. Residential):**
   * The current website promotes outdoor cane furniture and home wardrobes alongside industrial mobile compactors. Is leadership open to creating a dedicated B2B domain or commercial sub-brand (e.g. *D'furn Workspaces* / *D'furn Modular Systems*) to avoid brand dilution?
6. **USA Export & Hospitality Division:**
   * The catalog includes specialized Days Inn motel bedroom items. Is the USA hospitality export vertical currently active, and do you want inbound marketing to target US hotel franchisors, or should focus stay 100% domestic?
7. **Active Technical Certifications:**
   * Which formal certifications are currently valid and documented (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, BIFMA Level-2/3, GreenGuard)? These are mandatory for institutional university and GeM government tender bids.
8. **Internal Sales Staffing:**
   * Who will serve as the dedicated Inside Sales Coordinator responsible for meeting the <15-minute lead callback SLA, and who will handle 2D CAD space-planning layouts?
