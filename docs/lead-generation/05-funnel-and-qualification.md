### Lead Tier Action Routing:
* **Tier A+ (Score 85–100 · Priority Enterprise):** Immediate phone call within 15 minutes by Senior Business Development Manager. Dispatch physical Swatch Box via courier within 24 hours. Offer on-site space measurement or Kheda factory tour.
* **Tier A (Score 70–84 · High Commercial Intent):** WhatsApp engagement within 30 minutes. Request floor plan CAD/PDF. Generate 2D layout and formal BOQ quote within 48 hours.
* **Tier B (Score 55–69 · Standard Consideration):** Automated delivery of 2026 Digital Catalog via WhatsApp and email. Follow-up sequence initiated by sales coordinator.
* **Tier C/D (Score < 55 · Disqualified / Retail Query):** Automated message redirecting user to download standard catalog or visit partner dealer. Zero manual sales rep time invested.

### 3.2 Concrete Word-for-Word Scripts

#### Script A: Inbound Phone Qualification (First Call within 30 Mins)
> *"Hello [Name], this is [Rep Name] from D'furn Modular Systems in Ahmedabad. Thank you for requesting a commercial quotation on our website.*  
>  
> *I see you are planning a setup for [Workstation Count / Business Name] in [City]. To make sure we give you the most accurate factory-direct quotation: do you already have a 2D floor plan layout ready, or are you in the initial space-planning stage?*  
>  
> *(If CAD ready): Excellent. I am sending you a direct WhatsApp message right now. If you can share the DWG or PDF layout, our design team will optimize the seating density and share a formal itemized BOQ with 3D views within 24 hours.*  
>  
> *(If Planning stage): No problem at all. We manufacture modular steel-and-wood systems that easily expand. What is your approximate carpet area and target move-in date? Great, I will WhatsApp you our 2026 Workstation Lookbook and give you an estimated budget bracket today."*

#### Script B: WhatsApp Follow-Up after Quote Delivery (Touch 4 · Day 4)
> *"Hi [Name], [Rep Name] from D'furn here. Sharing a quick update regarding the workstation quotation we sent across on [Day] for [Company Name].*  
>  
> *Most corporate facility heads we work with find that our concealed raceway system and laser-cut steel understructures save approximately 20% in long-term maintenance compared to local carpentry.*  
>  
> *Did you get a chance to review the BOQ? Let me know if you would like us to tweak the screen partition finishes or adjust the seat counts. Happy to jump on a quick 5-minute call today."*

#### Script C: Handling the "Local Carpenter is Cheaper" Objection
> *"I completely understand, [Name]. On paper, a local carpenter might quote a lower upfront number. But here is what usually happens on commercial sites:  
> 1. Manual edge-banding begins peeling within 8 to 12 months due to moisture ingress, whereas our plant uses automated PUR/EVA edge-banding with zero glue-line.  
> 2. On-site carpentry causes 4 to 6 weeks of sawdust, fire hazards, and noise, risking site handover penalties. We manufacture 100% flat-pack in our Kheda factory and assemble on your site in just 3 days.  
> 3. We provide an authentic 5-year structural warranty backed by 30 years of manufacturing for clients like Reliance and ISRO.  
> Would you like me to send our physical Swatch Box to your office today so you can inspect the finish quality yourself before deciding?"*

#### Script D: The Graceful Breakup Message (Touch 7 · Day 16)
> *"Hi [Name], I haven't heard back regarding the workstation proposal for [Business Name], so I assume your project timeline has either shifted or you have selected another vendor.*  
>  
> *We are closing this active file for now so we don't bother you. If your expansion plans pick up again or you need additional cabin furniture, feel free to ping me here anytime. Wishing you the best with your new workspace!"*
